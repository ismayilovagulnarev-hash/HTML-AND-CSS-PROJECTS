Easy Tomato Pasta

A quick and delicious meal you can make at home.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Grated cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling water until tender.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and add the tomatoes.
  3. Add salt and pepper, then cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Mix the sauce with the pasta and add cheese.
